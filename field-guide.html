Our information standard

We distinguish verified facts, seller-provided claims, visible observations, and uncertainty. Photos and simple home tests cannot reliably identify every gemstone or metal. Avoid destructive tests; consult a qualified gemologist or metals professional when certainty matters.

Field note template
