# Welcome to Wild West Gemstones

### A Western Trading Post

Wild West Gemstones is a Western-inspired storefront built around the character of a trading post: a place where natural treasures, handmade creations, old curiosities, and botanical finds come together.

We bring together the rugged warmth of the American West, the natural beauty of stones and minerals, the character of antique and vintage objects, and the craftsmanship of handmade goods. Our visual direction draws on the feeling of discovering something unexpected in a small trading post, where every shelf holds a different story.

This repository documents our work in shaping that idea into a distinct web experience, from the original website-template reference to the storefront's design, structure, and implementation.

## Our Intent

Our intent is to create a digital Western trading post that feels welcoming, distinctive, and grounded in the things it represents.

We want the experience to invite curiosity and discovery rather than feel like a generic online catalog. Natural materials, earthy colors, gemstone tones, Western character, and the charm of collected objects guide our design decisions.

Wild West Gemstones brings several interests together in one setting:

- **Natural Stones:** The color, texture, and variety of the natural world.
- **Handmade Jewelry:** Craftsmanship, individuality, and personal expression.
- **Antique & Vintage:** Objects with character and a sense of history.
- **Botanical Goods:** The quiet beauty and variety of plant life.
- **Color Cabinet:** An exploration of color, materials, and visual inspiration.
- **Field Guide:** A place for discovery and learning.
- **Wholesale, Selling & Consignment:** Intended pathways for connecting goods with the people who value them.

Our goal is to make these different collections feel like parts of one place, united by a shared Western trading-post identity.

These categories describe the intended storefront experience. Their inclusion does not, by itself, indicate that every service or commercial feature is operational.

## Our Inspiration

Our inspiration comes from the atmosphere of the American West: trading posts, natural landscapes, mineral colors, weathered materials, antique silver, handmade objects, and the feeling of finding something worth keeping.

We draw on warm, earthy tones and a grounded visual palette, using gemstone colors and metallic accents for contrast. Rather than reproduce a historical storefront literally, we interpret its spirit for a modern web experience.

Discovery is central to the concept. We want the site to feel like browsing a thoughtfully assembled collection, where natural history, craftsmanship, and curiosity can exist side by side.

## Our Reference: Website-Template

Wild West Gemstones began with our original [Website-Template](https://github.com/VeraLynnDeGraw/Website-Template) repository as a structural and technical reference.

We used that web foundation as the starting point for developing a more specific concept. Our work focuses on shaping the storefront around its own identity, collections, content, and visual direction.

The relationship between the repositories is intentional:

- **Website-Template** is the original reusable web foundation.
- **Wild West Gemstones** is our project-specific adaptation, developed around the Western trading-post concept.

The purpose was not simply to reproduce the template under a different name. We used an existing foundation to develop a distinct digital experience with its own purpose and character.

The template is credited here as our starting reference. Specific implementation details should be understood in the context of the source files and project history.

**Original reference:** [Website-Template on GitHub](https://github.com/VeraLynnDeGraw/Website-Template)

## What It Is Built With

Wild West Gemstones uses standard web technologies and development tools:

- **HTML5** for page structure and content.
- **CSS3** for layout, styling, and responsive presentation.
- **Vanilla JavaScript** for client-side interactions.
- **Git and GitHub** for version control and project history.
- **Firebase Hosting** for hosting configuration and deployment.

Keeping the implementation close to standard web technologies makes the relationship between structure, presentation, and behavior easier to inspect and maintain.
